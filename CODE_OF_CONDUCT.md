# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FolioSkiff a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include showing empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment, publishing another person's private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with them. Moderation decisions should explain the reason when doing so does not expose a reporter or private information.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone officially represents the project, including repository discussions, issue trackers, reviews, release channels, and public events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abuse privately through the repository's GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting form and prefix the report title with `Conduct`. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer will acknowledge a report, assess context and impact, protect confidentiality as far as possible, and apply a proportionate response ranging from a private correction to a permanent ban.
